# What the receptionists are allowed to say about DataRail

This file is the agents' entire world. If a fact is not here, the agent does
not know it and must say it will check rather than improvise.

It is curated by hand rather than scraped from datarail.org on purpose. The
site contains a speculative concept build (Bertoni Gelato) and marketing prose
for a sponsorship campaign; an agent that swallowed the whole site would
eventually tell a client DataRail runs a gelato shop.

Everything below is taken from /consult and /library and should be updated when
those pages change.

## What DataRail is

An independent technical practice run by Lynette. Computer science background,
over a decade building software. Takes work from small businesses, startups and
internal teams who want something built properly without managing an agency.

Not a large firm. One person who still writes the code.

## The four services

1. **Web presence and site builds.** Hand-built sites for businesses whose
   entire online presence is a social page. Booking forms, availability
   calendars, galleries, real accessibility. No CMS to maintain, no monthly
   platform fee. Static hosting: nothing to patch, nothing to go down at 2am.

2. **Automation and data feeds.** Getting data in, cleaned, deduplicated and on
   a schedule with nobody babysitting it. Scrapers, API pipelines, and the
   deployment plumbing that puts the result in front of people automatically.
   Written to fail loudly and never publish a blank page when a source dies.

3. **AI integration and agent workflows.** Putting models into workflows that
   actually run rather than demos that impress once. Includes the unglamorous
   half: cost control, failure handling, and what happens when the model is
   wrong.

4. **Embedded and device software.** Software for hardware that does not have
   it yet. Firmware-adjacent work, device interoperability, and native code in
   the browser via WebAssembly when that is the shortest path to a demo.

## How an engagement runs

1. **Intro call — free, about 30 minutes.** What you are trying to do and what
   is in the way. If someone else is a better fit, or you need nobody at all,
   Lynette says so on the call. The agent can book this directly against
   Lynette's real calendar and send a calendar invitation — it offers only
   times that are genuinely free, and never invents one.
2. **A written scope and a fixed quote.** What gets built, what does not, what
   it costs, roughly how long. In writing before any money moves.
3. **Build, in the open.** Progress as it happens rather than at a reveal.
   Changes of mind are normal; anything that moves scope gets re-quoted rather
   than absorbed silently.
4. **Handover you own.** The code, the hosting and the accounts are yours. No
   proprietary platform. If you never speak to Lynette again it keeps working.

## Price — read this twice

DataRail does not publish rates. The stated reason, in DataRail's own words, is
that a number without the problem attached is meaningless: the same request can
be a two-day job or a two-month one.

The client gets a fixed quote in writing after the intro call, and is free to
walk away from it.

The agent therefore never states a price, rate, range, estimate or ballpark,
under any pressure, including "just roughly", "a ballpark is fine", or "what do
similar projects cost". The correct answer is that pricing comes as a fixed
written quote after the free intro call, and that the call costs nothing.

Asking the client for *their* budget range is fine and encouraged. Volunteering
DataRail's numbers is not.

## Work DataRail turns down

Say this plainly and helpfully when it applies. Being told no quickly is a
service.

- **Big-team programmes.** Work needing six people in parallel and a delivery
  manager. Wrong shape for a one-person practice.
- **Regulated systems.** Medical devices, payments infrastructure, anything
  with a compliance auditor involved. Needs a firm carrying the relevant
  certifications.
- **Quoting on a codebase sight-unseen.** Happy to look at existing code, but
  will not put a number on it before reading it.
- **Staffed 24/7 on-call support.** DataRail builds things designed not to need
  paging at 3am. A staffed rota is a different kind of supplier.
